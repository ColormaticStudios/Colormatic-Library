The Colormatic Forum is a platform for people to share creative projects, ask questions, and provide feedback & ideas. The purpose of this document is to describe intents, expectations, plans, and ambitions for the Colormatic Forum's leadership and community.

<!--more-->

### Bootstrapping

To get the Colormatic Forum started (bootstrapping process), we should have several tactics to invite new users and retain existing users once we have them.

**Obtaining users:**<br>
Word-of-mouth and having an enticing community with established high-quality posts is key.
- Create a Youtube video for the Colormatic Studios channel promoting the forum. Also make a more in-depth video with more details for the people who are interested.
- Ideally, members of Colormatic Studios should tell creative people about the forum and why they should show up. This can be online or in person.
- Some Colormatic Studios members should make a few posts (not too many) around the internet about the forum.

**Retaining users**:<br>
We just need to give people a reason to show up again.
- Recurring events: things like weekly prompts, monthly showcases, especially events that are unique to our platform.
- Gardeners: Core members that post projects in all communities to give potential users an example of what and how to post. Gardeners should also add welcome comments, feedback, constructive criticism, and helpful questions. They should gently model the kind of feedback and curiosity we are looking for.
- Responsive moderation: Users are unlikely to stick around if there is content that they don't want to see. Users will not like it if they report something and no action is taken.

**We are ready when:**<br>
- We have at least one gardener committed to the first month (ideally more)
- The stable release of Lemmy supports all of the features we need
- Each community looks inviting and has enough content to not feel empty (at least three posts, one comment).
- Every community has a banner image, pinned guidelines, detailed description, enough tags, and a good atmosphere.

### Communities

The forum will have communities that act as creative project "buckets" that posts can be funneled into. The list of provided communities is based around the goals, ideals and targets intended by the Colormatic Forum.

##### Guidelines for adding new communities and tags
Moderators should take careful consideration when deciding whether to add a new community or tag. Decisions should be made based on the values of the moderators, Colormatic, and the greater Colormatic community.

Communities should usually be added after user request, but prospective/experimental communities to see if there will be demand for an idea are acceptable.

Tags should be created when there is a common pattern of posts that does not fit any of the current tags in the community.

Communities and tags should not be created if they do not match the values of Colormatic and the greater Colormatic community. There must be enough demand for a community or a tag so that it will not be stagnant and mostly unused.

Communities and tags should not be created for brands, businesses or organizations. Communities and tags are intended to be generic and not controlled by a singular entity. For example, the crayon community should not have tags for specific brands of crayons, and we should not create communities for other creative-oriented companies or organizations. We may have a community for Colormatic, but that is an exception, not the rule.

##### Guidelines for moderators to tag posts
Tagging a post is, at its core; pattern matching. You are taking a post and matching it to whatever pattern it resembles most.

If a user does not tag their own post with the proper tags or uses tags that do not apply, it is the moderators' responsibility to tag it properly. If tagging becomes too time consuming for moderators, we may recruit members of the community as taggers to ease the load.

**Why there is no art community:**<br>
Short answer: art is ambiguous.

All of the creative projects on this platform are forms of art. The word "art" most often refers to human-generated images, (AI "art" is incorrect in my opinion), but can extend to storytelling, music, and more. For things like human-generated images, we have communities for drawing, painting, graphic design, etc. There is no art community because this whole website is an art community.

### User Guidelines

We expect our community to be respectful, welcoming, and open. Our community should promote peace and belonging, and should refrain from harassment, mobbing, dogpiling, blaming, spamming, doxxing, and impersonation. We should build a positive reputation among other communities around us.

Community members should only post projects that they are involved in (unless as a discussion), not take credit for other's work, and not post projects that aren't a creative endeavor (for example, AI generated content).

The Colormatic Forum is usually not the place to share other's work unless it is for a specific discussion. Things like news, meme dumps, etc. can happen somewhere else on the internet.

We encourage our community to have a presence on other platforms, but we ask that our community will be civil and positively represent themselves.

### Moderation

Moderation is a core aspect of a service that will make or break its success. In order for the Colormatic Forum to succeed, we must get moderation right.

The core purpose of the Colormatic Forum is for our community to have somewhere to gather, ask questions, and share projects and ideas.

At times, it may be necessary for us to dictate what is and isn't a creative project. As with many things in this world, there are obvious distinctions and nuanced edge cases. We shouldn't be quick to make a judgment of this nature, but we must be willing to do so when it is needed. This type of decision generally shouldn't be done alone and moderators should discuss with peers before settling on a judgment.

Moderators have often had a rather negative reputation on other platforms on the internet. This is a valid concern that we must address. Our moderators should be humble and understanding but also decisive and effective.

The main goal of moderation on the Colormatic Forum is to create a safe and inviting place for people to discuss and share creative projects. hostile comments, disturbing content, and spam will drive away new and existing members.

We still do want to harbor the regular community chatter and banter that keeps communities alive. Moderators should not take their job too far as to silence the community. Valid criticism, both positive and negative, are critical components to keeping leadership in check.

Moderation decisions should not upset the community unless absolutely necessary and reasonable community members agree. If a decision unexpectedly upsets the community, Colormatic Studios should seriously consider walking it back.

Sometimes, a decision made by a moderator will be obvious to the moderator and maybe some community members, but for one reason or another won't be obvious to the user(s) who are affected. Users should be given a reasonable amount of information regarding moderation actions so they can understand. Explanations from other community members are acceptable, but the moderator must provide one otherwise. If a user asks for too much, a moderator may cut them off.

Moderator actions must be monitored by other internal members to ensure honesty and proper intentions. This includes all community members that have been granted extra permissions.

Colormatic's reputation is very important and it is critical for moderators to respect and uphold it.

### Internationalization

The Colormatic Forum should not be limited to one language. The goal is to serve everyone who wants to join and will get along with the community.

As soon as we can, we should create an automatic translation feature for users to be able to understand posts that were not written in a language they know. However, we recognize the difficulty of building such a system so it may not be available on launch. Ideally, this system will be built into the UI and the user can just click the menu on a post and select a "translate" option. A bot that can be summoned to translate a post is less ideal but can help fill the gap until an ideal system can be implemented.

Colormatic as a project is not local to any particular language, region, or ethnicity. Colormatic is meant to be provided to everyone without any discrimination or personal selection. While translation and internationalization is difficult and expensive, we recognize it as a fundamental part of Colormatic.

### Independence

Independence is a core principle of Colormatic. We do our best to not rely on any third parties, and if we do, we make them as replaceable as possible. What this means for the Colormatic Forum is that we will try not to rely on any single third party for our operation, and if we do, they should be as replaceable as possible.

This does not mean that we will disable the federation feature in Lemmy or refuse to partner with third parties. Federation is very useful for discoverability and cross-internet interaction, and partnerships are very important for building trust, recognizably, and connections. However, it is important for us to maintain our agency over the Colormatic Forum.
